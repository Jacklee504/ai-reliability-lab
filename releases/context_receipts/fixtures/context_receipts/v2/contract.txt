SERVICE AGREEMENT SUMMARY
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2025-01-15

1. Fees and Payment
The monthly service fee is 2,800 EUR per calendar month.
Invoices are issued on the first working day of each month.
Payment is due within 15 days of the invoice date.
A late payment interest of 1.5 percent per month applies to overdue amounts.

2. Term and Termination
The agreement runs for an initial term of 12 months.
Either party may terminate the agreement for convenience with 60 days written notice.
Termination for cause requires 30 days written notice and an opportunity to cure.
After termination the customer must return or destroy all copies of the software.

3. Data Protection
Personal data is processed solely for the purpose of delivering the service.
The processor must notify the controller of any personal data breach within 24 hours.
Data is stored within the European Economic Area.
Data processing takes place only in Ireland.

4. Liability
Total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 24 months preceding the claim.
The cap does not apply to intentional misconduct or breach of confidentiality.
Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.
Liability for data protection breaches is capped separately at 500,000 EUR.
